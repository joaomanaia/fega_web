# FEGA Web

Community platform born in Ega, Condeixa — designed to scale to any locality, region, or worldwide community.

## Language

**Post**:
A public message published to the community feed, optionally with images or a link preview.
_Avoid_: publication, article

**Group**:
A named community with members and a dedicated realtime chat.
_Avoid_: channel, room

**Group Message**:
A message sent inside a Group, delivered in realtime to its members.
_Avoid_: chat message, group post

**Event**:
A time-bound gathering with a location, description, and detail page; discoverable via list and map.
_Avoid_: activity, occurrence

**Event Map**:
The geographic view of Events filtered by date range and location.
_Avoid_: events map view

**User**:
An authenticated person with a profile, username, and membership in Groups.
_Avoid_: account, member (member is a User in the context of a Group)

**Feed**:
The chronological, paginated stream of Posts on the home page.
_Avoid_: timeline, wall
